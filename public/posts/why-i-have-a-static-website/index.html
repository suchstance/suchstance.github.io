Why I Have A Static Website

Nov 16, 2026

The World Wide Web was built as a way to manage information. An elegant design containing URLs, HTTP and HTML. That was all you needed to open any page on the internet, using a device that can access it. Much like our minds, we can hop between topics connected by links and browse down rabbit holes as much as we can. It’s simple enough for many people to build their own page and express themselves without bureaucracy.

I write this in the autumn months of 2026, when expressing myself is easiest on any of the social media platforms I use, but impossible to share with someone who is not in the same network as the platform. I am increasingly annoyed that social media is no longer ‘world-wide’ - and I want my art to be seen by anyone with a device, without an advertisement, a pop-up or a login screen.

This is yours to see as much as it is mine to share.

I had fun building this website over the last few months, on and off. I learnt a tonne

  1. I learnt that I had to plan my information architecture fairly well before getting started
  2. I also learnt that my stylistic choices were questionable; an older version of this website was bright yellow and teal and completely unreadable
  3. I made silly mistakes like mis-type the extentsion for images and nothing got rendered
  4. I got better at Markdown and HTML
  5. And I got better at previewing on localhost before git push
